bit of a personal post this time. for well over a decade, i have been playing rhythm games (Etterna, Spin Rhythm, Beat Saber, A Dance of Fire and Ice, etc.) and other precision games (Geometry Dash, Kaizo SM64 ROM-hacks, I Wanna Be The Guy, etc.). i was never particularly a top player in any of these games aside from A Dance of Fire and Ice, but my skill level was above average in every game

prior to last night, it had been months before i'd really tried playing any of these games with performance in mind. sometimes during work breaks i'd hop on one of these games and just play for a little bit, not really paying attention to my skill level. last night, however, i decided to use my gaming equipment for the first time in over a year and i immediately noticed that i was nowhere near how i used to be

i was talking to one of my buddies, and he we had a conversation that went something like this:

![](https://i.postimg.cc/qMYgvyGK/text.png)

so, i continued playing for another hour or so. no difference at all. after that, i decided to just call it quits

![](https://i.postimg.cc/Bvvq5vpR/text.png)

would i still be as good if i didn't take a roughly year-long hiatus from gaming? probably. can i do anything about it now? probably not. life is really busy and i don't have enough room to devote much of my time to video games. it's unfortunate, but it happens

if by some miracle i manage to regain some (if not most) of my skill in these fields, i will make a follow-up post. until then, you'll see me yap here about other things in the meantime. that's all i really needed to get off my chest bye lol
